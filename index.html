How it works

Placement
A sponsored card inside the conversation. It is labelled, supplied by the host and never presented as a model answer.
Watch
A sponsored message. The player pauses when you leave the tab or the card leaves the view.
Verify
The host checks the ad, duration and freshness, then signs a single use receipt.
Credit
The ledger adds 40 free credits once. Replays, short watches and session caps are refused. Sponsored credits apply to Sage Mini.

Demo issuer. Fictional sponsor. No network. Production needs a server side verifier and an ad network proof. Read the protocol ↗
